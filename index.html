Who We Are

We started this because too many Australian authors were being talked into things they didn't understand. Vanity presses, hidden fees, contracts that quietly took their rights. Our approach is simpler: figure out what your book actually needs, tell you what it costs, and let you decide.

You'll know who's working on your book, what stage it's at, and what happens next. No account managers reading from a script. No surprise charges on the invoice.

We're not a manuscript mill. We're editors, designers and publishing people who take on a limited number of books at a time, and we treat each one like it matters — because to the author, it does.

If you value keeping control of your work and knowing exactly what you're paying for, you'll probably get along with how we do things.
